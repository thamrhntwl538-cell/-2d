# M16 rendered visual review

Reviewed all 19 M16 viewport captures covering all ten rooms, including the laboratory maintenance floor and the archive's four playable elevations. The matched M15 images remain alongside them under `previews/m16/before/`; the final M16 images are under `previews/m16/after/`.

The new room paintings and plain stone surfaces leave more quiet space around the Warden. Repeated gilded arches and platform filigree no longer compete with movement. Computer desks, terminals, notice papers, shelves and the tribunal's code display retain the university identity. The cold, dark architecture and limited warm/cyan sources remain coherent across the stage.

The unchanged M13 lantern and ivory cloak stay readable at all inspected positions. Platform lips remain visible on the lower laboratory floor, the archive balconies and the darker approach/tribunal edges. No empty camera margins, missing skins, large light washouts or hero-obscuring foreground objects were found in these captures.

The initial OUTPUT capture exposed one composition issue: an upper platform hid most of the suspended core. The final capture moves only that decorative prop below and to the right of the platform. Its body and chain are now clear, and the playable floor remains open. Only the OUTPUT before/after pair was recaptured; the other 18 pairs retain their original evidence.

Native Godot 4.6.3 with the Compatibility renderer produced 38 retained images. All 19 before/after collision and exit-requirement signatures match. Each M16 fixture stayed within its camera bounds and used the original 30×58 collider, 0.4 art scale and fixed foot pivot. The visible lighting cap stayed at three local pools plus the hero's light.

The image measurements in `M16_VISUAL_QA.json` are advisory: sampled background brightness and edge activity decreased in every matched view. They support this review and do not certify artistic quality. These captures use labelled checkpoint fixtures for inspection; the independent actual-input traversal results are recorded in `M15_ENVIRONMENT_QA.json`.

No visual blocker remains within the inspected positions. Enemy/boss combat and user acceptance of the art are outside this review.
