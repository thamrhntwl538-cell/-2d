LAST LECTURE — M23 original music and audio

Original title: The Unfinished Proof / البرهان غير المكتمل

1. Original material

The composition, arrangement, MIDI files, procedural combat cues, and Python
composition/rendering source were created specifically for LAST LECTURE in
October 2026. No Hollow Knight recording, melody, MIDI, audio asset, or game
data was used. The original contributions are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

To the extent possible under law, the author has waived all copyright and
related or neighboring rights to those original contributions. They may be
used, modified and distributed commercially without attribution. The sample
library notice below remains applicable to the rendered orchestral recordings.

Original procedural audio, using no external samples:
  compiler_bolts.wav, compiler_sweep.wav, compiler_pillars.wav, compiler_phase.wav
  All new wordless character voices, movement, combat, interaction and ambient
  WAVs listed in character_audio_manifest.json are also original procedural
  audio under the same CC0-1.0 terms. Voice synthesis uses a glottal source,
  original formant/pitch envelopes and generated breath noise. It uses no
  person-specific voice model, reference recording, extracted video sound,
  external voice actor sample or cloned vocal identity.

Original scores rendered with the MIT-licensed FluidR3 GM instrument samples:
  compiler_phase_1.ogg, compiler_phase_2.ogg, compiler_victory.ogg
  compiler_phase_1.mid, compiler_phase_2.mid contain the original score only.

3. Rendering dependencies

The rendering tools use libfluidsynth, NumPy, SciPy and FFmpeg as external
build tools. They are not copied into this game. Godot uses the supplied Ogg
and WAV files directly and does not require a soundfont or Python at runtime.
