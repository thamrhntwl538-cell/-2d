1. Original material — CC0-1.0

The musical composition, arrangement, note timings, supplied MIDI files,
composition/render source and original mastering decisions were created for
LAST LECTURE. No Hollow Knight recording, melody, MIDI, sound effect, game data,
commercial music recording or external score was used as composition material.
The two files are:

3. Rendering dependencies and review scope

NumPy, SciPy, libfluidsynth and FFmpeg are external rendering/measurement tools;
they are not packaged in the game. The soundfont is not packaged in the game.
Only rendered Ogg files, the original MIDI/source and these notices are included.
Godot plays the Ogg files directly without a soundfont or Python dependency.
No human listening review is claimed. Objective codec, loudness, peak, clipping,
loop-seam and runtime lifecycle results are recorded alongside these files.
